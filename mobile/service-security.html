What is the best way to make my front door more secure?

Start with the lock. Fit an anti-snap cylinder (TS007 3-star or Sold Secure Diamond) on a uPVC or composite door, or a BS 3621 mortice lock on a wooden one. Then add a viewer, chain or limiter so you can check who is there before you open the door.

FAQs

Questions about security upgrades

Anything else? Call 07958 033395 and ask.

What is lock snapping?+

A way of breaking some euro cylinders in seconds. A TS007 3-star or Sold Secure Diamond cylinder is designed to resist it.

Are smart locks secure?+

Good ones are. We fit smart and keypad locks that carry recognised security ratings, and keep a key option.

Is the security check free?+

Phone advice is free. If we come out to look at your doors, we tell you any charge before we book the visit.

Will an upgrade lower my insurance?+

It can help you meet your policy wording. Ask your insurer what they need and we will match it.

Do I need planning permission for security bars or a new door?+

Not usually for locks, chains or viewers. If you live in a conservation area and want a new door or bars on the front, check with your council first.

Are anti-snap locks worth it?+

Yes, on uPVC and composite doors. Lock snapping is a common way in, and an anti-snap cylinder is a quick, low-cost fix.
