How we work

Our promise to every customer

Whatever the job and whatever the time, this is how we work.

Price agreed firstWe give you the price on the phone before we set off, and nothing changes without your say-so.
Damage-free entry firstWe try non-destructive entry first. If a lock has to be drilled, we tell you before we start.
Repair before replaceIf a repair will do, we will not sell you a new lock. You only pay for what the job needs.
Insurance-approved locksWe fit BS 3621 mortice locks and TS007 3-star anti-snap cylinders to match your policy.

Tested before we leaveWe test the lock with you, hand over the keys and show you the worn part if we replaced it.
