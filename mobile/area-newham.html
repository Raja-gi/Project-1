Local know-how

Terraces, shared houses and Royal Docks flats

Long Victorian and Edwardian terraces run through Forest Gate, Upton Park, East Ham and Manor Park. Most still have wooden front doors with a mortice lock and a nightlatch, and many are now flats or shared houses.

Around the Royal Docks, Canning Town, Custom House and Beckton the homes are newer: blocks of flats with fire-rated entrance doors, and family houses with uPVC doors.

Almost four in ten Newham households rent privately (Census 2021), so a lot of our work here is for tenants, landlords and letting agents.

Shared houses

Locks for shared houses in Newham

Newham licenses most private rented homes, and shared houses (HMOs) have their own fire safety standards. These are the lock jobs Newham landlords ask us for most.

1Bedroom doorsCouncil HMO standards usually ask for room locks that open from the inside without a key, so nobody is trapped in a fire. We fit thumb-turn locks and key each room differently.
2The front doorThe way out has to open from the inside without a key too. We fit a lock with a key outside and a thumb-turn inside.
3When a tenant leavesWe change the cylinder on their room and the front door, so old keys stop working. It takes 20 to 40 minutes on most doors.
4Records for your licenceYou get an invoice with the property address and what we fitted, ready for an inspection.

Letting property in Newham?

Property licensing in Newham

Newham was the first council in England to license every private rented home, in 2013. Its current selective licensing scheme started on 1 June 2023, so most rented homes in the borough need a licence.
