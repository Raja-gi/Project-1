Try this first

Forgotten code or dead keypad?

A few things to try before you call. None of them will harm the safe.

1Change the batteriesMost keypad faults are flat batteries. Many safes have an outside battery point or a backup key.
2Stop after a few wrong codesMany safes lock you out for a while after repeated wrong codes. Wait, then try again.
3Find the model and serial numberThey are often on a label or in the manual, and help us bring the right parts.
4Have proof it is yoursWe ask for ID and proof that the safe is yours, or that you are allowed to open it.

Can a safe be opened without damaging it?+

Often, yes. We try non-destructive methods first and only drill if there is no other way, then repair the safe afterwards.

I have forgotten my combination. Can you help?+

Yes. We open the safe and reset or reprogram the combination.

Do you repair electronic safes?+

Yes. Keypad faults, flat batteries, wiring faults and failed solenoids.

How long does it take?+

Simple resets and repairs usually take under an hour. Opening a high-security safe takes longer.

Do you open safes for businesses?+

Yes, in shops, offices and homes across East London.
