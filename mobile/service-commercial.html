Master keys

How a master key system works

One key for the owner, one for each team, one for each door. Nobody carries more keys than they need, and a lost key means changing one door, not every door.

Grand master
Grand master: The owner or manager. Opens every door.
Master 1Master 2
Master: A department, floor or shop. Opens all the doors in that area.
Individual 1Individual 2Individual 3Individual 4
Individual keys: One person or one room. Opens only that door.
