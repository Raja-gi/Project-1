Cookie policy

What cookies our website uses and how you can control them.

Last updated November 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files saved on your device when you visit a website. They help the website work and show us how people use it.

2. Cookies we use

Essential cookies keep the website working, for example remembering your cookie choice. They cannot be turned off.

Analytics cookies tell us which pages people visit, so we can improve the website. We only set them if you agree.

Advertising cookies help us measure our adverts. We only set them if you agree.

3. Your choices

When you first visit, you can accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your mind at any time with the cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the website may not work without them.

4. More information

Find out more about cookies at ico.org.uk. Questions: email info@eastlondonlocksmith.co.uk.
