Why will my uPVC door not lock?

Most often the door has dropped, so the hooks and rollers no longer line up with the keeps, or the gearbox inside the lock has worn out. Both are usually repaired without a new door or a whole new lock.

How to measure your euro cylinder

Two numbers and a photo mean we arrive with the right parts.

1Find the fixing screwOpen the door and look at its edge. The long screw in the middle of the faceplate holds the cylinder.
2Measure each sideMeasure from the centre of that screw to the outside end of the cylinder, then to the inside end. Sizes are written like 40/50.
3Read the faceplateThe brand is often stamped on it, such as Yale, Era or Fullex. It tells us which gearbox fits.
4Do not force the handleIf it will not lift, stop. Forcing it is how gearboxes break.

FAQs

Questions about uPVC doors and windows

Anything else? Call 07958 033395 and ask.

My uPVC door is stuck locked. Can you open it?+

Yes. A failed gearbox can lock the door shut. We open it, usually without damage, and replace the gearbox.

Do you need to replace the whole multipoint lock?+

Often not. Most problems are a worn gearbox, a handle or an adjustment.

Do you fix composite doors too?+

Yes. Composite doors use the same kind of multipoint locks.

Are my uPVC door locks anti-snap?+

Many older euro cylinders are not. We can fit a TS007 3-star or Sold Secure Diamond cylinder.

Can you repair a composite door lock?+

Yes. Composite doors use the same kind of multipoint locks, gearboxes and cylinders as uPVC doors.

Do you fix uPVC windows?+

Yes. Window handles and locks that are stiff, loose or will not lock.

Should I use WD40 on my uPVC lock?+

Use a PTFE or silicone spray on the moving parts. Some cylinder makers ask you not to use WD40 or graphite in the keyway.
