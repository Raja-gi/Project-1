FAQs

Questions about prices

Anything else? Call 07958 033395 and ask.

Are these prices for the part or the whole job?+

The whole job. Every price is supplied and fitted: the lock, fitting and labour, with no hidden extras. We confirm your price on the call before we come out.

Why do you cost more than a lock from a DIY shop?+

Our price includes fitting and labour, and we only fit branded, Kitemarked locks from reputable UK locksmith wholesalers. Cheap unbranded locks can wear quickly and fail.

What is the difference between a 1-star and a 3-star cylinder?+

A Kitemarked 1-star cylinder passes British Standard tests against picking, drilling and bumping, but it needs 2-star security handles to be anti-snap. A 3-star cylinder is anti-snap on its own, so it is the one we recommend for front doors.

Can I get a fixed price?+

Yes. Call us or send a photo of the lock and we confirm the price before anyone sets off.

Do you charge more at night?+

We confirm the full price on the phone before we come out, whatever the time, and it does not change when we arrive unless you ask for extra work.

How can I pay?+

Card, cash, Clearpay or Klarna when the job is done. Clearpay and Klarna: 18+, T&Cs apply.
