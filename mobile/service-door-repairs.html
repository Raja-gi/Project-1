FAQs

Questions about door repairs

Anything else? Call 07958 033395 and ask.

Why will my door only lock when I lift the handle hard?+

Usually the door has dropped or the keeps are out of line. That is normally an adjustment, not a new lock.

Can you repair a door frame after a break-in?+

Yes. We repair split frames and fit new keeps, and can board up first if the door is not secure.

Do you fix communal and fire doors?+

Yes. We repair closers, hinges and locks on shared entrances. Fire doors are repaired with fire-rated parts.

Do I need a new door?+

Rarely. If it is beyond repair we will tell you and give you a price, but most doors can be fixed.

Can you fix a door that has dropped?+

Usually, yes. Most uPVC and composite doors have adjustable hinges, and wooden doors can be rehung.

Why does my door stick in summer?+

Doors expand in the heat, and uPVC most of all. If it only happens when it is warm, an adjustment usually fixes it.
