New-build flats

Moving into a flat in Stratford or East Village?

East Village, the former London 2012 Athletes’ Village, and the blocks around it are some of the newest homes in East London. These are the questions we hear most from people moving in.

1Who else has a key?Previous tenants, agents and contractors may still have one. Changing the cylinder takes 20 to 40 minutes on most doors.
2Your flat door is a fire doorMost entrance doors in these blocks are fire doors with euro cylinders. We fit a cylinder that keeps the door to its rating.
3Ask the building firstIf you rent, your landlord or the building’s management may need to agree. We can invoice them directly.
4Locked out of the buildingTell us when you call. We arrange access with the concierge or management office, then open your flat door.
