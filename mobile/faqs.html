Getting help

How quickly can you get to me?+

Usually within 30 to 60 minutes anywhere in East London. We give you an arrival time when you call.

Are you open 24 hours?+

Yes. Every day of the year, including nights, weekends and bank holidays.

Which areas do you cover?+

Newham, Tower Hamlets, Hackney, Waltham Forest, Redbridge, and Barking and Dagenham. If you are just outside, call and ask.

What ID do I need?+

Photo ID and proof that you live at or are responsible for the property, such as a utility bill or tenancy agreement. If it is inside, we check it once the door is open.

Prices and payment

How much does a locksmith cost?+

It depends on the lock and the door. Our prices are all-inclusive, supplied and fitted, from £75 for a nightlatch barrel. See our prices, or call and we confirm the price before we come out.

Do you agree the price first?+

Yes. We give you the full price on the phone before we set off, and nothing changes without your say-so.

How can I pay?+

Card, cash, Clearpay or Klarna. Clearpay and Klarna: 18+, T&Cs apply.

Do you invoice landlords and businesses?+

Yes, with your reference and the property address. Ask us about paying on account.

Locks and security

Will you damage my door or lock?+

We try non-destructive entry first. If a lock has to be drilled, we tell you why and agree the price before we start.

Can a lock be repaired instead of replaced?+

Often, yes, and it usually costs less. If it cannot, we tell you why and give you the price for a new one.

How long does a lock change take?+

Most euro cylinder changes take 20 to 40 minutes. Mortice and multipoint locks take longer.

Do you fit insurance-approved locks?+

Yes. BS 3621 mortice locks and TS007 3-star or Sold Secure Diamond anti-snap cylinders.

When should I change my locks?+

After moving in, losing keys, a break-in, a change of tenants or staff, or when a lock is worn or not up to your insurer’s standard.

Landlords, businesses and safes

Do you work for landlords and letting agents?+

Yes. Tenancy changeovers, eviction lock changes and repairs reported by tenants.

Do you work for businesses?+

Yes. Shops, offices and warehouses, planned or 24 hours, with master key systems and shutter repairs.

Can you open a safe if I have forgotten the code?+

Yes. We open the safe and reset or reprogram the combination.
