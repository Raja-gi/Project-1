FAQs

Questions about our services

Anything else? Call 07958 033395 and ask.

Can I pay with Clearpay or Klarna?+

Yes. As well as card and cash, you can spread the cost of bigger jobs with Clearpay or Klarna in interest-free payments. 18+, T&Cs apply.

Do you charge a call-out fee?+

Our prices are all-inclusive: the lock, fitting and labour, with no hidden extras. We confirm the price on the phone before we come out.

Will you damage my door to get me in?+

We try non-destructive entry first. If a lock has to be drilled, we tell you before we start and fit a new one there and then.

Do you fit insurance-approved locks?+

Yes. We fit British Standard BS 3621 mortice locks and TS007 3-star anti-snap cylinders. Check your policy wording and we will match it.

Can you make one key open all my doors?+

Yes. We can fit keyed-alike locks so one key opens every door, or cut keys to match the locks you already have.

Do you work for landlords, letting agents and businesses?+

Yes. Tenant changeovers, eviction lock changes, master key systems, shutters and shop or office locks. Ask us about paying on account.
