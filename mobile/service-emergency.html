Proof of address

Have this ready: proof it is your home

A genuine locksmith always checks you live there before opening a door. Any one of these is usually enough.

Secured by Design advises using a locksmith registered with the Master Locksmiths Association
1Photo ID with your addressA driving licence with the address on it.
2Post or a billA bank statement, council tax bill, utility bill or tenancy agreement with your name and the address.
3On your phoneA council tax email, your bank app or an online account showing the address usually works.
4If it is all insideThat is fine. We open the door and check your documents inside. If you rent, your landlord or agent can confirm you live there.
GOOD TO KNOWWhile you wait for us+
1Stay somewhere safeA neighbour, a café or your car. We will call you when we are close.
2Do not force the doorShoulder-barging or using a card can damage the lock and the frame, and makes the job take longer.
3Find your proof of addressA bank app, a council tax email or a delivery on your phone all help.
4Tell us about the doorWooden, uPVC or composite, and whether it is just shut or locked with a key.
5Call 999 if anyone is at riskA child, a vulnerable person or a pet shut in on their own, or anything dangerous.

FAQs

Questions about emergency locksmith

Anything else? Call 07958 033395 and ask.

How quickly can you get to me?+

Usually within 30 to 60 minutes anywhere in East London. We confirm an arrival time when you call.

Will you damage my door?+

We try non-destructive entry first. If a lock has to be drilled, we tell you why and agree the price before we start.

Do you charge more at night?+

We give you the full price on the phone before we set off, whatever the time. Nothing changes without your say-so.

What if my ID is inside?+

That is fine. We open the door and check your ID or post with your name and address before we leave.

Do you charge a call-out fee?+

You get one full price on the phone before we set off. There are no surprise extras when we arrive.

What should I check before booking any locksmith?+

Ask for the price before anyone sets off. A real price on the phone, not a call-out fee “plus whatever the job turns out to be”. Ask about non-destructive entry. Most lockouts do not need a drilled lock. Ask why if someone says yours does. Check the company is real. A company name you can look up, real reviews and a phone number that is answered. Pay by card if you can. Card payments can be disputed. We take card, cash, Clearpay and Klarna.
