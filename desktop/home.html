Quality locks, not cheap imports

Not all locksmiths use the same locks. Some fit cheap unbranded locks that can wear quickly and fail. We do not.

Common questions

Anything else? Call 07958 033395 and ask.

How quickly can you arrive?−

We aim to be with you within 30 to 60 minutes. We'll confirm an arrival time when you call.

How much does a locksmith cost in East London?+

It depends on the lock and the door. Every price is supplied and fitted, with no hidden extras: for example a 3-star anti-snap cylinder from £130. We confirm your price on the phone before we come out.

Do you work on weekends and bank holidays?+

Yes. We're open 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Are you insured?+

Yes. East London Locksmith Ltd is fully insured.

Can you replace locks the same day?+

In most cases, yes. Tell us your door and lock type when you call and we will confirm before we set off.

Can I pay with Clearpay or Klarna?+

Yes. As well as card and cash, you can spread the cost of bigger jobs with Clearpay or Klarna in interest-free payments. 18+, T&Cs apply.
