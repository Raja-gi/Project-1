FAQs

Questions about getting in touch

Anything else? Call 07958 033395 and ask.

Do you have a shop I can visit?+

No. We are a mobile locksmith, so we come to you, wherever you are in East London.

How quickly will you call me back?+

Send a request and one of our locksmiths will call you back in a couple of minutes. If you are locked out, call us instead for the fastest help.

Are quotes free?+

Yes. Quotes are free and there is no obligation.

Can I book a time that suits me?+

Yes. For lock changes, upgrades and planned work we will agree a time with you.

Do you invoice landlords and businesses?+

Yes. We send invoices with your reference and the property address. Ask us about paying on account.

How can I pay?+

Card, cash, Clearpay or Klarna when the job is done. 18+, T&Cs apply.
