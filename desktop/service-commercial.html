Master keys

How a master key system works

One key for the owner, one for each team, one for each door. Nobody carries more keys than they need, and a lost key means changing one door, not every door.

Can you work outside opening hours?+

Yes. We can work early, late or at weekends so your business keeps trading.

What is a master key system?+

A set of locks where each person has a key for only the doors they need, and managers have one key for all of them.

Do you repair shutters?+

Yes. We repair roller shutter locks and mechanisms, and can secure a shopfront the same night.

Do you work for managing agents?+

Yes. Communal doors, bin stores and plant rooms in blocks of flats.

Do you offer business accounts?+

Ask us. We invoice your accounts team with your PO number or site reference.

Can you secure a shop after a break-in?+

Yes. We change locks, repair shutters and doors, or board up the same night.
